# Off on the charger, its screen gone dark: an update over USB lights it,
# shows the update, and the remote stays off; it goes dark again after
click Start
wait 300
tab Settings
wait 1500
click Power off
wait 300
click Power off
wait 500
usb on
wait 11000
expect-log power: off, charging, screen dark
mark
usbfw 30
wait 300
expect Updating over USB
expect 30%
# lit for as long as it runs, past the charging screen's 10 s
wait 15000
expect-log-no power: off, charging, screen dark
usbfw fail
wait 300
expect Update stopped
wait 6500
expect-no Update stopped
wait 11000
expect-log power: off, charging, screen dark
