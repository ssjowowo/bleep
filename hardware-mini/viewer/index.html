How these were made

Each mini has its own layout.py, shell (remote.scad) and routed main board, derived from v1 and checked by independent audits (hardware-mini/mini28, hardware-mini/mini24). The keys, windows and corners follow v1's design; the panels are BuyDisplay parts from AliExpress and the cells come from Ampul. X-ray shows the board, the cell, the module and the panel inside each shell. The screens show the emulator's Home tab at each panel's own resolution (240×320 on the minis).
