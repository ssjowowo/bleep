# Bleep mini28 — shopping list, with prices

Generated by `../tools/shopping.py` from the BOM (`mainboard/fab/mini28-mainboard-BOM.csv`), JLCPCB's assembly files (`mainboard/fab/jlc/`) and the prices in `../research/prices.json` (fetched 2026-10-11 by `../tools/prices.py`; 1 USD = 0.8924 EUR, ECB). Prices and stock change: check each link before you pay.

**The plan:** JLCPCB makes the boards and assembles the **top side** with **Economic PCBA**; you solder the underside (the ESP32 module and the few parts next to it, the USB-C shell legs, the motor wires) with an iron. Quantities and costs are for **2 remotes** (JLCPCB makes at least 5 bare boards and assembles 2 of them, so 3 stay bare as spares; LCSC rounds small parts up to their minimum order). JLCPCB and LCSC prices are US$ without VAT; AliExpress, Ampul and skroutz prices are € with 19 % Cyprus VAT, and each AliExpress line adds ≈ €3 import charges. §7 has the totals in € with VAT; §8 compares this plan with the alternatives.

- **This plan costs ≈ €37 more than building everything by hand** (≈ €19 a board), and saves the reflow kit (paste, a 200 mm hotplate or an oven: ≈ €90–320) and the fine-pitch top-side work. Economic's fees are small: $8.18 setup, $1.53 stencil, $1.01 for the joints and $39.91 for the 13 Extended parts (Basic and preferred parts cost nothing extra).
- Standard two-sided assembly would cost ≈ €89 more, mostly its fixed fees (two-sided setup $51.12, stencils $16.42, fixture $16.42, feeder loading $56.61).
- The hand-build column prices the same part numbers at LCSC; a few of the Basic parts JLCPCB uses have no loose stock at LCSC, so a hand build would use the equivalents in `../research/parts.md`.
- JLCPCB's parts include a small attrition allowance (+5 per passive line); the assembly fees come from JLC's published price table (updated 2026-09-09). Upload the BOM + CPL to get JLC's exact quote.
