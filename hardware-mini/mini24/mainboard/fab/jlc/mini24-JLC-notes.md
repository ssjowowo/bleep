## Order settings

- PCB as in the README: 2 layers, **0.8 mm, green solder mask, lead-free HASL** (at 0.8 mm JLC offers Economic assembly only with green + HASL), qty 5 (JLC's minimum); then **PCB Assembly** with **PCBA qty 2** (the remotes being built; the other 3 boards stay bare as spares).
- **Recommended: Economic PCBA, top side only.** Upload the plain fab Gerbers (`mainboard-gerbers.zip`), `mini24-BOM-JLC-top.csv` and `mini24-CPL-JLC-top.csv`, assembly side **Top**. Every top-side part is "Economic and Standard" at JLC and none has the X-ray flag (the mic is the MSM261DHT006 for that reason). Economic reflows at a fixed 255 ± 5 °C. You then solder the underside by iron: J2, Q4, R28, U1, plus the motor wires, as in the README.
- Alternative, **both sides** (Standard only: two-sided assembly and the ESP32 module are Standard-only): upload `mini24-gerbers-JLC.zip` (it adds the bottom paste layer), `mini24-BOM-JLC.csv`, `mini24-CPL-JLC.csv`, side **Both sides**.
- Unique parts: 35 (16 Basic/preferred, 19 Extended); top-only: 16 Extended. Economic charges $3.07 per unique Extended part per order (Basic and preferred: nothing); Standard charges $1.53 per unique part.
- **Order remark** (paste it):

  > D1/D2 VSMB2948SL (C6604342) are SIDE-VIEW IR LEDs: the lens must face the board's top edge (silk arrows), cathode = the pad marked K. U9 is a top-port MEMS microphone: no washing / ultrasonic cleaning, do not cover or seal the port.

## Check in JLC's placement preview before paying

Every row below was checked by placing JLC's own footprint where the CPL row says (pin for pin against our copper, worst error ≤ 0.5 mm, see the table), so the preview should show every part on its pads. Look anyway at:

1. **D1 and D2 (VSMB2948SL)**: JLC has no footprint for this part, so their engineer places it from the remark. The lens (the dome on the side of the body) must point at the board's top edge (the end with the IR LEDs), cathode on the pad marked K. If the preview shows them turned, reply in the DFM review; don't change the CPL.
1. **J1 (FH34SRJ ZIF)**: the flip lid hinges at the back, over the contact pads (towards the board's top end); the FPC opening faces the keys (+y in KiCad, the fold side), as in the README's assembly steps. JLC's footprint matched ours pin for pin with contact 1 at +x, so a lid shown the other way round in the 3D view would be a 3D-model problem: ask in the DFM review rather than turning the part.
1. **J2 (PicoBlade, bottom)**: the plug opening faces towards the board's BOTTOM (USB-C) end, i.e. down towards the cell below it (README §14: J2 left of the module, 'mouth towards the cell'), the two solder tails on the opposite side. JLC's footprint is the KiCad one turned 180°, corrected in the CPL. Remember JLC's bottom view is mirrored: the board's left edge appears on the right.
1. **U1 (ESP32-S3 module, bottom)**: antenna end over the board's right edge seen from the top (on the left in JLC's mirrored bottom view, as the underside silk "<- antenna" says), pin 1 at the silk pin-1 mark. The CPL point is the centre of JLC's footprint (the pad field), not the module body: if the preview shows the module shifted ~3.6 mm along its length, JLC used the body centre; their engineer fixes such offsets in the DFM review. The EPAD gets paste through the library's 9 windows; the iron-soldering holes in it may wick a little solder through to the top (harmless; top them up from the top if one stays empty).
1. **U9 (MSM261DHT006)**: pin-1 corner as on the silk (the dot); orientation sets where the port lands under the shell's gasket (the port is 1.42 mm from the pad-7 end of the lid), so it must match. It is rated for 5 reflows at 260 °C; hot air on the port is not allowed.
1. **Y1 (FC-135)**: non-polar; fine either way round.
1. **J3 (USB-C)**: mouth at the board edge. Its 4 shell legs sit in plated slots with no paste aperture: solder them yourself from the underside with the iron (with the other underside work).

## Checks run

- 80 placements = every footprint with an LCSC part that isn't DNP (92 footprints on the board, 12 left out as listed).
- Every BOM line has an LCSC number; every CPL centre lies inside the board outline (outline polygon, window excluded); layers from the footprints' sides; bottom SMD pads all carry B.Paste.
- Coordinates: the Gerbers' frame (KiCad absolute, y up), the same frame as the fab Gerbers and position file.
- End-to-end: JLC's footprint for each LCSC part placed at the CPL row's position and rotation lands on our pads pin for pin (worst pad-centre error ≤ 0.5 mm; the small residuals are pad-size/pitch rounding between the libraries).
