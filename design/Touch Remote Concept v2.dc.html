DIY TOUCH REMOTE · CONCEPT PACK v2 · MODULE BUILD

Touchscreen universal remote

Slim bar with a 3.5″ touch screen on top and 14 hardware keys below. Built from an off-the-shelf ESP32-S3 3.5″ display board, a few breakout modules and one through-hole key PCB, with a 2000 mAh Li-Po and USB-C charging, in a 3D-printed FDM enclosure. Controls IR devices directly and everything else through Home Assistant over Wi-Fi.

Drawn at 3 px per mm at the original custom-PCB size. The module build in section 04 needs a larger shell, about 66 × 196 × 20 mm.

Key PCB
2-layer, through-hole only, no SMD parts. 13 switches, a 6-pin header for the harness, a footprint to solder the MCP23017 module flat on the back, 2 pads for the PWR switch wires and 3 M2 mounting holes. Rows and columns go straight to the MCP23017 pins, so there's no diode matrix.
