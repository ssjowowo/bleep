DIY TOUCH REMOTE · CONCEPT PACK v1

Touchscreen universal remote

Slim bar with a 3.5″ touch screen on top and 14 hardware keys below. Built around an ESP32-S3 on a custom PCB, a 2000 mAh Li-Po and USB-C charging, in a 3D-printed FDM enclosure. Controls IR devices directly and everything else through Home Assistant over Wi-Fi.

01

Industrial design, three directions

Same internals in all three (3.5″ display, 50 × 60 mm battery). The PCB is sized for 1b. Drawn at 3 px per mm.

58 × 178 × 16 mm

Flat slab with an even thickness. The battery sits behind the key area. Two flat shells, so the visible faces print without supports.

Check before ordering the display
The 1b shell leaves about 52.8 mm inside. Many 3.5″ modules are 54–56 mm wide. Pick a panel 52 mm wide or less, or widen the body to about 59 mm.
